Substack Slop Filter Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Substack Slop Filter is a browser extension that filters Substack feed items and can show profile statistics based on Substack's visible labels.

Information processed

On Substack pages, the extension reads page content, including visible labels, profile identifiers and article links, to hide matching items and calculate optional profile statistics. It also reads the current page URL. This processing takes place in your browser. The extension does not send this information to the developer or any third party and does not use analytics or advertising.

Storage and deletion

The extension saves your filter and display preferences using browser extension storage on your device. It does not save the content it reads from Substack as browsing history or a profile database. Preferences remain until you change them, clear the extension's data, or uninstall the extension.

Sharing and limited use

The extension does not sell or transfer user data. Its use of data is limited to providing its filtering and profile-display features and complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other services

Substack and your browser operate under their own privacy policies. This policy covers the extension itself, not those services.
