Substack Slop Filter: Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026. Applies to version 1.6.0 and later.

Substack Slop Filter is a browser extension that hides Substack feed items carrying Substack's "Fully AI-assisted text" or "Not scanned" labels and can show a count or percentage of labeled posts beside a profile name. This policy explains what the extension accesses and what it does with it.

What the extension accesses

  • Substack page content. On substack.com and *.substack.com pages, the extension reads the page to find Substack's AI labels and hide matching feed items.
  • Substack profile data. When profile statistics are turned on and you open a profile page (substack.com/@handle and its Activity, Posts, Replies and Likes tabs), the extension requests that profile's public information and its most recent posts (up to 100) from Substack's own servers, using your existing Substack session in the browser. It reads each post's author and AI-label fields to calculate the statistic.
  • Profile identifiers. To make those requests, the extension reads the profile handle from the page address and the numeric profile ID returned by Substack.
  • Your settings. The extension stores your filter and profile-display choices with the browser's extension storage.

What the extension does with it

  • All processing happens in your browser. Profile statistics are kept in memory in the open tab for up to 10 minutes and are discarded when the tab is closed or reloaded.
  • The extension sends no data to the developer or to any third party. Its only network requests go to Substack, the site you are already using.
  • The extension does not collect, sell, share or transfer user data, does not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending purposes, and does not use it for any purpose unrelated to its single purpose.
  • The extension does not change your Substack account, does not post or subscribe on your behalf, and does not perform its own AI detection. Labels come from Substack.

Remote code

The extension does not load or execute remote code. All of its code ships in the installed package.

Removing data

Removing the extension deletes its stored settings. Profile statistics are never written to storage.
